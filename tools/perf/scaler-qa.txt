Note the final state: ratio 1.458 sitting ABOVE its own climb cap of
hotRatio * HOT_MARGIN = 1.53425 * 0.9 = 1.381. That is precisely the state
that used to be TERMINAL — the up-branch refuses any step that would not
raise the ratio, so before this ticket the session was pinned at 1.458 for
good, whatever the machine did afterwards. Reproducing it here in a real
browser is the bug, not a leftover.

What lets it out is the latch relaxation (render/resolution.ts,
RELAX_AFTER_MS): 60 s of UNBROKEN clean frames lifts hotRatio one notch and
the climb resumes, with the required clean period doubling at each relaxation
so probing gets geometrically rarer instead of pumping. This 30 s window is
deliberately too short to reach that — the workload here is one the GPU
cannot hold, so it never produces a clean minute. Recovery is proven instead
by client/test/resolution.test.ts (five tests that fail against the shipped
controller) and by simulating the real module: ~15 min of clean frames back
to the full device ratio after a boot hitch, ~55 min from the floor.
